# Odds Whisper — demo video script (up to 3 minutes)

Film this in the Even Hub simulator on the borrowed desktop, or on G2
glasses if you have them. Read the lines aloud in plain words, at a
steady pace. Do not rush the tap — let the lens change on screen.

One sentence a judge should repeat afterwards: **"Tap your temple and
the glasses whisper the crowd's odds."**

## Hook options — pick one, say it aloud, keep the clearest

1. "Tap. Sixty-two percent say yes." (lens already showing the odds)
2. "I never open my phone to check a market again."
3. "What does the crowd think right now? My glasses just told me."

## 0:00–0:05 — Hook (sound off: it must work with no audio)

On screen before you say anything: the simulator lens, already showing
a live claim — the market question and a big Yes %.

On-screen text (large, bottom third): **TAP TEMPLE. SEE THE ODDS.**

Say: "Tap your temple, and the lens tells you what the crowd thinks.
This is Odds Whisper."

## 0:05–0:20 — Problem, one sentence, one named user

Say: "Ada trades on Polymarket, but every time she wants the odds she
has to stop, pull out her phone, open the app, and search. She misses
the moment while she is looking down."

On-screen text: **ADA CHECKS THE ODDS ON HER PHONE — AND MISSES THE MOMENT.**

## 0:20–2:00 — The one live flow (about 90 seconds)

Film the simulator window full screen. Narrate what is happening, not
the labels on screen.

1. Say: "Watch. I tap my temple." Tap once in the simulator. The lens
   changes to the next claim.
2. Read the lens aloud: the market question, then the Yes percent, then
   the check line — volume, 24-hour move, end date. Say: "That is the
   crowd's odds, live from Polymarket, and one check line so I know if
   the market is moving."
3. Tap again. Read the second claim aloud, shorter this time: question
   and Yes percent only.
4. Tap a third time. Say: "Every tap is the next market. Nothing to
   type, nothing to search."

If the lens shows DEMO instead of a live claim, say so on camera, plainly:
"This one says DEMO — the live fetch did not reach Polymarket, so these
are baked-in example odds, not real ones." Never read a DEMO claim out
as if it were live odds.

## 2:00–2:30 — How it works (30 seconds)

Say: "Underneath, it is simple. The app reads the public Polymarket
Gamma API — no account, no key, read-only. A temple tap is a click
event, and the lens refreshes just the text, so there is no flicker.
Closed markets and finished results are filtered out, because a price
of zero or one hundred percent is a result, not odds."

On-screen text: **POLYMARKET GAMMA API · NO KEY · READ-ONLY**

## 2:30–3:00 — What's next (30 seconds)

Say: "Next I want a tap-and-hold to pin one market I care about, and a
small alert when its odds move fast. Odds Whisper is built on the Even
Realities SDK for Track 02, Polymarket."

On-screen text: **ODDS WHISPER — EVEN REALITIES TRACK 02: POLYMARKET**

End on the lens showing a claim. Stop recording.

## Honest limits — say them if asked, do not hide them

- The app has never run in the simulator or on real glasses yet. The
  first simulator run is the test: if the lens says DEMO, the live
  fetch is blocked and needs fixing.
- Nothing is stored, nothing is traded, no wallet, no money moves.
  The app only reads public odds.

## Recording notes (phone / borrowed desktop)

- Turn on Do Not Disturb on the phone first, so no notification lands
  mid-take.
- Film in landscape. Browser zoom 100–125%, full screen, bookmarks bar
  hidden, extra tabs closed.
- Use the phone's built-in screen recorder if filming the phone, or the
  desktop recorder for the simulator window. Microphone on.
- Record one long take of about 3 minutes, then trim two edits in
  CapCut (free): a judge cut at 60–75 seconds and a social cut at
  25–35 seconds, from the same footage.
- Add captions — the video may be watched without sound.
- Record a separate 60-second insurance capture of the tap working, so
  a dead network on submission day still leaves something to submit.
- Never show a real API key on screen. (This app has none.)
- Rehearse out loud at least five times before the take you keep.
